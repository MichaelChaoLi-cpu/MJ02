# Cambodia–Thailand Gate C: first NPP-only main-model results

Status: **model-dependent negative continuous-dose interaction. The human-approved 1,000-draw random-pair Monte Carlo passes its directional rule and both sector-specific continuous-dose estimates are negative, but the predetermined fixed distance-ring diagnostic fails: every ring coefficient is small and positive, the joint p-value is 0.7249, and the inner-minus-outer contrast is essentially zero. The continuous result is therefore not robust to relaxing its linear distance functional form and is not ready for causal promotion.**

Human approval record: `MILI-D-20260822-014`

## Frozen first-opened specification

- Primary outcome: annual land NPP anomaly in kg C per m2.
- Primary hazard: positive May–October rainfall-deficit intensity.
- Pre-conflict period: 2001–2007.
- Excluded escalation and conflict period: 2008–2011.
- Post-conflict period: 2012–2024.
- Exposure: continuous 2011 conflict dose, declining from one at a verified event to zero at 60 km.
- Fixed effects: 1 km grid cell and climate-cell × year.
- Weights: frozen outcome-independent Gate B overlap weights.
- Reported covariance: 10 km spatial-block clustered and debiased.
- Target: conflict dose × post-conflict × drought intensity.

No bandwidth, sample, outcome, or functional-form search was conducted after the target
coefficient was opened.

## Main results

| Specification | Target estimate | Clustered SE | 95% CI | p-value |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Primary: natural-unit NPP × dry-rainfall intensity | -0.04313 kg C/m2 | 0.02096 | [-0.08422, -0.00205] | 0.0396 |
| Scale check: standardized NPP × dry-rainfall intensity | -0.66014 | 0.31439 | [-1.27633, -0.04395] | 0.0358 |
| Mandatory confirmation: natural-unit NPP × long dry-spell intensity | -0.03487 kg C/m2 | 0.01758 | [-0.06933, -0.00041] | 0.0474 |

All three target estimates are negative and pass the conventional two-sided 5% model-based
threshold. The main sample contains 742,080 grid-cell-years, 37,104 grid cells, 1,380
climate cells, and 455 frozen 10 km spatial blocks over 20 included years.

## Interpretation

The primary coefficient compares an event-location conflict dose of one with a zero-dose
location. After 2011, a one-unit increase in rainfall-deficit intensity is associated with
an additional 0.043 kg C/m2 NPP loss at that full-dose contrast relative to the pre-conflict
difference in drought slopes. One unit of the primary drought measure is a one-standard-
deviation seasonal rainfall shortfall when rainfall is below its climate normal.

The weighted mean 2001–2020 NPP level on the frontier support is approximately 0.732 kg
C/m2, so the full-dose coefficient is about 5.9% of mean annual NPP. This is an endpoint
contrast, not the average effect among all exposed cells. Because the weighted mean dose
among exposed cells is 0.159, applying the linear model at that dose implies about 0.0068 kg
C/m2, or 0.9% of mean annual NPP, per one-unit drought shock.

The negative standardized and dry-spell estimates confirm direction and conventional
significance without changing the sample to seek a favorable result.

## What is and is not established

The first-opened model supports the empirical statement that post-2011 drought sensitivity
is more negative where the constructed conflict dose is higher. It does not yet establish a
robust causal effect of conflict because the treatment is generated by two conflict sectors
and conventional block-clustered standard errors can overstate independent treatment
replication. Both primary and mandatory-confirmation p-values are close to 0.05.

The first three sequential validations are now complete. The 1,000-draw random-pair Monte
Carlo places the actual estimate in the lower 3.4% tail under the approved directional rule.
The sector decomposition produces negative estimates in both sectors and a nonsignificant
cross-sector difference (p = 0.724), but neither reweighted sector-only estimate is
individually significant and the Ta Moan/Ta Krabey standalone sample has weak covariate
balance. The fixed-ring diagnostic then fails the expected distance-decay pattern: the four
ring coefficients are 0.0056, 0.0080, 0.0078, and 0.0030 kg C/m2 and jointly have p = 0.7249;
the inner-minus-outer contrast has p = 0.9807. The script exactly reproduces the original
continuous coefficient on the same sample, so this is a functional-form discrepancy rather
than a data mismatch. The remaining approved diagnostics are:

1. sensitivity of uncertainty to 5, 10, and 20 km spatial blocks; and
2. early-, medium-, and late-post-conflict drought-slope estimates.

These tests can characterize the stability of the continuous association, but cannot by
themselves repair the absent ring-based distance gradient. They must be reported regardless
of outcome, and no alternative ring boundary should be selected after this result.
